incerto

A research terminal for crypto markets, built as a personal project. It pulls data from CoinGecko, Hyperliquid, and FRED, keeps the raw data around so results can be reproduced, and has a paper-trading engine and a daily BTC regime model on top. Still very much in progress.

research

  • Is Retail Dead?

  • Ethereum Congestion and the Mean Reversion of DEX-CEX Price Spreads

    Empirical research on whether Ethereum congestion slows the correction of DEX-CEX mispricing, showing that higher gas weakens price alignment when arbitrage is actively operating.

    [Paper]
  • When does LVR Outrun Fees?

    A brief research empirical note on AMM liquidity provision, LVR, and toxic flow. The core result is that fee income rises in more toxic regimes, but LVR rises faster.

    [Paper]

    Some thoughts on the ways in which institutions incentivize inaccurate forecasting, embedded in a reflection of Nate Silver's The Signal and the Noise.
